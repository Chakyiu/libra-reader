LibraReader

A quiet place to keep what you mean to read. Two prototypes below share one set of tokens, one type pairing, and the same sample library of design criticism, systems thinking, and urbanism.

Primary · desktop

The connected app

Eight routed views — Home, Library, Reader, Highlights, Collections, Collection detail, Search, Settings — sharing one state object, so selections and filters survive as you move between them.

Open the app Companion · mobile

The phone

A 390×844 frame with bottom navigation, single-column library, full-screen reader with an auto-hiding bar, and bottom sheets for adding content, reading settings, and annotations.

Open the phone

Wired end to end

  • Highlight, then annotate

    Select any sentence in the reader, pick a colour, attach a note, and find that passage waiting under Highlights — click it to return to the exact spot in the article.

  • Add a URL

    Paste an address, watch it fetch, review the preview, choose a collection, and save to the Inbox. Duplicate links and paywalled pages each get their own answer.

  • Archive with Undo

    Finish an article, archive it from the end of the page, and the confirmation stays long enough to change your mind.

Both files are standalone — no build step, no dependencies beyond the two webfonts. Reading preferences and the last view are kept in localStorage. Palette and type decisions are recorded in brand-spec.md.
